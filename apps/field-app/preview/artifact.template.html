What you are looking at

These are the real screens, the real stylesheet and the real offline queue, from packages/field-ui. The only thing replaced is the network: every call is answered from a fixture instead of All Star's database, which is what lets it run here without a rep's password.

The same code renders at all-star.bi.letlois.com/field in a browser. There is one copy of these screens, not two.

Worth trying

Tap "Go offline", then log a knock. Pick an address and an outcome and hit the log button. It saves instantly, the counter on Today ticks up, and the pill at the top says the knock is on the phone.

Tap "Go offline" again and the queue drains on its own. That is the whole design: a rep at a door never waits for signal, and a knock is never lost because there was none.

The three screens

Knock
Where a rep spends the shift. Outcome buttons big enough for one thumb in the sun, the street remembered between doors, and contact fields that only appear when the outcome implies somebody to follow up with. Tap Use my location to see the doors the team already worked, including one household that asked not to be knocked again.
Today
Everything logged, queued knocks first, with a small mark for whether it has reached the office yet. Notes stay editable for a day after the door.
Me
The rep's own numbers, today and over the week. Managers also see the team's. The figures here are invented but weighted the way the work actually goes: most doors reach nobody.

What differs on a real phone

Location
Comes from the OS rather than the browser, and prompts once. Only while the app is open, and optional: decline it and every knock can still be typed.
Sign in
A rep signs in once with the account their manager made. The session is held in the phone's own storage, not a cookie, and it stays signed in day to day.
The queue
Also mirrored into native storage, so clearing the app's cache cannot take a rep's unsent day with it.
Icon and name
All Star Field with the door mark, on the home screen. The mark is generated and ready to be swapped for whatever All Star want on their listing.

Before it can be downloaded

The build is done and the code is on the branch. What is left needs a person rather than a commit: Apple and Google developer accounts, which take days to verify; a signing key; screenshots from a real device; the privacy policy hosted at a public URL; and a demo rep account, without which Apple will not review an app they cannot sign into.

The full runbook is in docs/field-app-release.md.
