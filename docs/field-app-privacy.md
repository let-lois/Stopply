**Effective TODO. Applies to the All Star Field app on iOS and Android.**

All Star Field is a work app. It is used by All Star's field
representatives to record the doors they knock during their shift. It is not
available to the public: an account is created by a manager, and there is no
way to sign up inside the app.

The app is built and operated by LetLois on All Star's behalf. All Star
decides what is collected and what it is used for; LetLois stores it and
keeps it working.

## What the app collects

**Your account.** Your name, work email, phone number if your manager
recorded one, and the team you are on. Your manager creates this; you cannot
change it in the app beyond your password.

**The knocks you log.** For each door: the address, the outcome you chose,
any notes you write, and the time. If the household gave you their name,
phone number or email for a follow up, whatever you enter.

**Your location, if you allow it.** When you tap to use your location, the
app reads your device's position and attaches it to that knock. It is used
to show which doors on a street have already been worked, to warn you before
you knock a house that asked not to be, and to let your manager see where a
day happened.

The app asks for location **only while it is open**. It does not have, and
does not ask for, background location. It does not track you between knocks,
during breaks, or after your shift.

**Nothing else.** No advertising identifier, no analytics, no crash
reporting service, no contacts, no photos, no microphone, no third party
tracking of any kind. The app talks to one server, All Star's own dashboard
at `all-star.bi.letlois.com`, and to nothing else.

## What it is used for

Your knocks are the reporting All Star reads: how many doors were worked,
how many conversations happened, what came of them. Your manager can see
your activity and your team's. This is a record of work, and it is used the
way a record of work is used, including in how performance is assessed.

It is not sold. It is not shared with advertisers or data brokers. It is not
used to build a profile of you outside your work.

## People at the door

When you record a household's name, phone number or email, that person has
given it to you so All Star can follow up with them. It is used for that.

If a household asks not to be knocked again, that is recorded against the
address and it does not expire. Anyone at the household can ask All Star to
delete what is held about them, or ask what is held, by writing to TODO.

## Where it is kept, and for how long

On your phone, a knock is saved before it is sent, so you can keep working
with no signal. It stays on your phone only until it reaches the server,
then it is removed from the phone.

On the server, it is stored in All Star's own database, separate from every
other LetLois client, hosted on Cloudflare in the United States. It is kept
as long as All Star keeps it as a business record. Ask All Star how long
that is; LetLois does not decide it.

Your session is stored on your phone in the operating system's own app
storage, which no other app can read. Signing out removes it.

## Your choices

**Location.** Decline it, or turn it off later in your phone's settings. The
app keeps working and you can log every knock by typing the address. Nothing
is hidden from you if you decline.

**Your account.** Your manager creates and removes accounts, in the
dashboard, so ask your manager to close yours. You can also write to TODO
and we will pass it on. Closing your account stops your access immediately.
The knocks you logged stay, because they are All Star's record of work done,
not a copy of your personal data.

## Children

This is a work app for employed representatives. It is not directed at
children and no one under 16 should be using it.
